## What this does and does not show

- **Shown:** on these fresh seeds the candidate completed 154 more final-bank attempts than the
  reference in total: 1,884 against 1,730.
  - It won two of three training-seed pairs and lost one by a large margin.
  - Its per-level coverage was broader: 30 against 26 levels at 50% or more, and 16 against 26 never
    cleared.
  - Across the two rounds where it was tested (lowlr-1020 and this one), its final-bank totals were
    1,770 and 1,884 of 5,940 (29.8% and 31.7%). ff12's were 1,569 and 1,730 (26.4% and 29.1%).
- **Not shown:**
  - that 512×2 at lr 0.0003 is reliably better: one of three fresh pairs went the other way, and the
    registered rule failed;
  - statistical significance;
  - competent gameplay.
- **Seed spread** dominates the comparison. The reference ranged from 516 to 642 final-bank clears
  across these three seeds, and the candidate from 513 to 714.
- **Throughput confound.** The candidate trained 28.2–28.8M steps here (about 7.8–7.9k steps/s),
  fewer than in lowlr-1020 (31.9–32.8M). The reference trained 30.6–34.0M. Machine load differed:
  analysis and gallery work ran alongside parts of this round. Scheduling and behaviour-dependent
  simulation cost could both contribute, and the design does not isolate them.
